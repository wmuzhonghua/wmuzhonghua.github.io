**中文摘要（归纳）**

研究开发了无需衍生化的亲水作用液相色谱–串联质谱（HILIC–MS/MS）方法，结合稳定同位素内标、一步蛋白沉淀及解冻后立即加入抗坏血酸稳定样品，可在 12 分钟内定量 15 种血清神经递质及相关代谢物。验证显示，各分析物的 R² 均大于 0.99，检出限为 0.01–0.30 ng/mL，回收率为 85.19%–107.41%，日内和日间精密度可接受。抗氧化处理减少了易氧化分析物的损失。在 188 名孕妇中，与非抑郁对照相比，产前抑郁与 γ-氨基丁酸升高及乙酰胆碱、胆碱、去甲肾上腺素、左旋多巴、5-羟色胺和 3-甲氧基酪胺降低相关。该平台支持血清神经递质谱分析及孕期生物标志物研究。

**English Abstract (Summary)**

The study developed a derivatization-free hydrophilic interaction liquid chromatography–tandem mass spectrometry (HILIC–MS/MS) method to quantify 15 serum neurotransmitters and related metabolites within 12 minutes, combining stable isotope-labelled internal standards, single-step protein precipitation, and immediate post-thaw serum stabilization with ascorbic acid. Validation showed R² > 0.99, detection limits of 0.01–0.30 ng/mL, recoveries of 85.19–107.41%, and acceptable within-day and between-day precision. Antioxidant protection reduced losses of oxidation-sensitive analytes. Among 188 pregnant women, antenatal depression was associated with higher gamma-aminobutyric acid and lower acetylcholine, choline, norepinephrine, L-dopa, serotonin, and 3-methoxytyramine than in non-depressed controls. The platform supports serum neurotransmitter profiling and pregnancy biomarker research.

研究采用孕鼠高剂量机制性暴露模型，通过组织学、高光谱成像及同位素标记代谢组学，比较 30 nm 和 120 nm 炭黑纳米颗粒的肺部效应。两种粒径均引起母体肺炎症和纤维化损伤，其中 120 nm 颗粒造成的损伤更重；两者均经胎盘转运进入胎肺。胎肺未出现明显组织学损伤，但存在氧化应激和炎症变化。30 nm 颗粒主要影响组氨酸代谢，120 nm 颗粒则扰动丁酸和花生四烯酸代谢通路，并引起更广泛的胎肺代谢变化。结果表明，母体与胎儿的反应具有粒径差异，胎肺即使没有可见结构损伤，也可出现生化紊乱。

Using a high-dose mechanistic model in pregnant mice, the study compared the pulmonary effects of 30-nm and 120-nm carbon black nanoparticles through histology, hyperspectral imaging, and isotope-labeling metabolomics. Fetal lungs showed oxidative stress and inflammatory changes without overt histological injury. The 30-nm particles mainly affected histidine metabolism, whereas 120-nm particles disrupted butanoate and arachidonic acid pathways and produced broader fetal metabolic changes. These findings indicate size-dependent maternal and fetal responses and fetal biochemical disturbances even in the absence of visible structural damage.

The findings suggest that disruption of these pathways may contribute to impaired sperm motility and spermatogenesis, providing molecular evidence for associations between environmental exposure and sperm quality.

**中文摘要（归纳）**

这项前瞻性研究随访了灌口老龄队列中 1,881 名至少 55 岁的成年人，中位年龄为 61 岁，并用生存分析、Cox 模型及限制性立方样条评估胆红素相关的高血压风险。总胆红素、非结合胆红素及结合胆红素的风险比分别为 1.03、1.05 和 0.96，相应的 95% 置信区间为 1.01–1.05、1.03–1.07 和 0.92–0.99。三者均表现出非线性关系，转折点分别为 12.17、8.59 和 3.47 μmol/L。基线总胆红素结合年龄、体质指数及腰围有助于区分较低和较高的高血压风险。结果显示，不同胆红素组分的关联存在差异，结合胆红素的总体关联方向与其他两者相反。

**English Abstract (Summary)**

This prospective study followed 1,881 adults aged at least 55 years in the Guankou Ageing Cohort, with a median age of 61, and examined bilirubin-related hypertension risk using survival analysis, Cox models, and restricted cubic splines.
